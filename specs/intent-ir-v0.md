# Intent IR v0

Status: scaffold

## Purpose

Define the small, typed intermediate representation generated by a planner and consumed by deterministic software.

## Planned concepts

- Route: `local`, `hybrid`, `external`, `clarify`, `defer`, or `deny`
- Ordered steps referencing declared capability identifiers
- Typed arguments and references between step outputs
- Preconditions, postconditions, timeouts, and retry limits
- Approval and privacy annotations
- Explicit uncertainty and reason codes

## Design constraint

The IR must remain smaller and safer than arbitrary generated code. Exact fields and invariants will be finalized before implementation.
